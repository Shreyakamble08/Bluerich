Shipping Policy

1. Order Processing

  • Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of confirmation.
  • Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

2. Delivery Timelines

  • Metro cities: 2–3 business days.
  • Tier 2 & 3 cities: 3–5 business days.
  • Remote areas: 5–7 business days.

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to unforeseen circumstances such as weather, strikes, or courier delays.

4. Delivery Address

Please ensure your shipping address and contact details are accurate. We are not responsible for failed deliveries due to incorrect information provided at checkout.

5. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your order arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photos and your order ID. We will investigate and take appropriate action.

6. Contact

For any shipping-related queries, reach out to us at support@bluerich.in or WhatsApp +91 72197 13151 .

All shipments are subject to courier partner availability. We are not liable for delays caused by factors beyond our control.
